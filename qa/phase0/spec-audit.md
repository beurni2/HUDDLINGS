### The 23 refuted findings, with the verifier's reason

- **Head yaw is clamped to ±20° but the view swap fires at 22°, and the three-quarter covers only 30-60°: no drawing covers about 18-30°** Misread. The ±20° is the fake-turn channel's range on the front drawing, not a clamp on the head's logical yaw.
- **The M0 'hatch skeleton' runs on device before the R12 age screen (M1), before the hatch/hello clips (Gate D), and before the name filter (M3)** This is an engineering scoping matter with an obvious reversible answer, not a blocker. L937 already limits the hatch to the dog before M5, and 'skeleton' signals placeholders.
- **Playback variation (amplitude up to +12%) and the ear-perk time push motions past the rig's own limits and the face timing rule** The rig's limits are hard limits stored in rig.json (L417, scale 0.93–1.07; L421, tilt ±18°; Appendix F, L2432 (head) `"rot": [-18, 18]` and L2429 (body) `"squash": [0.93, 1.07]`). QA's Stretch row flags any channel that leaves its range (L692), so variation is necessarily applied inside the limits, either by clamping or by authoring keys with headroom.
- **Core motion 9 'tips over onto its back' needs a pose no sheet draws; a screen-plane tip and new belly-up art give different builds** The spec already decides how to handle a pose the parts cannot make: 'If a motion cannot look right with the current parts, add a view, a state or a part, redraw and retry' (L827), and 'a pose that only works by stretching or bending a part gets a new state or view instead' (L580). The founder approves every core motion at Gate C (L733), and the Lab's wedge view (L679) exists to compare variants.
- **Gate A asks for four view stills, but six view-facings are built, and two of them (right-facing sitting side, right-facing three-quarter) have no sheet crop for the silhouette check** Already decided. The contradictions table (L302) says to keep both side facings and that 'the marking map uses each for its own side'.
- **The dog's rigging sheet labels left and right from the viewer, while the spec's .L/.R are the companion's own sides; not covered by the decided table** The spec already pins the convention twice: L367, and Appendix F (L2406, '.L has a positive x'). The rigging sheet is trusted only for 'which parts exist and how each may move' (L283).
- **R13's rig-completeness and visual-QA build failure fires on every PR from M0 to M5, while the plan is dog-only until M5** Refuted: the spec already phases the check. L738: 'The dog passes A to C in M0, and D for Home and Treats by the M2 gate.'
- **Copy lint: whole-word matching lets inflections like misses, died, Treats and feeding through, but stem matching would fail the canonical 'Mission complete'** Refuted: the spec picks one matching rule and one list. L85 and L1933 say 'matches them as whole words, ignoring case', and the list spells out the inflections it wants (miss/missed, starve/starving, forgot/forgotten, die/dying).
- **Copy lint scope is undefined for '!', the literal 'Mochi', data-driven strings and server-built text** Refuted: the scope is already 'every user-visible string' (L85, L1933). That list names notification templates, which cover server-built push copy, and link-preview text, which covers the edge worker's tags.
- **The jar's standing hint 'Find a real banana for {mochi}' conflicts with one-time hints and the no-nudge rules** Refuted: the hard rules already decide it, and they rank above section 7. P9 (L1112): 'No notification, badge, timer or repeated hint ever asks anyone to send, reply, feed Mochi or come back'.
- **{time} in link share text is the sender's local time, read by group-chat members in other time zones** Refuted as decided and low-consequence. L1936 defines `{time}` as 'a local time in the phone's own format'.
- **R13 speciesOnly counterparts can't be one-to-one: bear and rabbit have two species idles, dog and kitty one** Refuted. The vocabulary (L214) lists exactly which species-only idle acts exist for each companion, and R13 and L208 treat those differences as deliberate exceptions ('one species' own on purpose'). 'with its counterparts for the other three' (L91) only asks that each entry name the clip filling its role in each other species.
- **A quick second tap is both show-off and the paw's high five, and it eats rapid boops, so touch traces can't be labelled** Refuted. The show-off row (L871) already sets the precedence.
- **Scene listening animations play on the hills behind a 97% opaque note, so Royal's speakers, the Hug lean and the Secret Agent poof can't be seen** Refuted for most of its claims. The spec puts the listening visitor on the note itself: 'the visitor sits at the bottom edge of the note and listens along' (L1235), and 'the visitor at the bottom edge, listening along' (L1390).
- **A web reply on the sender's Home 'performs the reply's scene', but nothing says whether the sender must do the scene's action** Refuted. L1418 says the sender's own Mochi 'performs the reply's scene itself', so Mochi is the performer and the sender is the audience.
- **Next-up and pending links promise an arrival time that nothing defines** Refuted. Section 8's rules are deterministic enough to derive the time.
- **Up to three waiting visitors plus your own Mochi go beyond the memory and CPU budgets, which cover only one visitor** Refuted as a blocker. The budgets at L716-717 constrain only 'one companion' and 'with a visitor', so a three-visitor state breaks no stated rule, and visual QA's Performance check (L709) has nothing to fail it on.
- **Block can't keep a blocked person's links away, because link resolution carries no identity and Block needs an account** Refuted. Appendix C lists only body fields.
- **The first treat plays 'the peel hat', but the hat isn't earned, and whether it stays on or a keepsake moment plays is undefined** Refuted. L1006 decides it.
- **Analytics consent is undecided and contradicts the age-screen and opt-out statements** Refuted. The default is decided: 'People can turn them off in Settings' (L2071) means the setting is on by default, as an opt-out, the same way Sounds and Vibration are.
- **Delete everything: scope, mechanism and aftermath undecided, and it resets blocks and a Mochi Post pause** Refuted. Scope: the test plan settles it, 'Delete everything removes the account's rows and files within 24 hours' (L2162), as do Appendix C, 'everything within 24 h' (L2373), and the veil copy, 'Your Huddling, your friends and anything on its way go too.' (L2046).
- **The web page stores identifiers for readers it knows are under 13; COPPA treatment is not on counsel's list** Refuted. The finding says this is not on counsel's list, but it is.
- **Complete file protection is impossible for files the Lock Screen camera writes while the phone is locked** Refuted; the quote is misread. L1582's complete-protection rule covers temporary files 'in the app's temporary folder', the folder AVAssetWriter uses in the main app (L1578), which records only while the phone is unlocked: locking stops recording (L1546).
