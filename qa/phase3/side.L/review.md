# The side view: fresh reviewers' scores

Three fresh reviewers, one lens each, scored the side view against its turnaround crop (spec 5b's rubric, 1 to 5; every line must reach 4 before Gate A). They had not seen earlier rounds.

| Reviewer | On-model | Face | Charm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Character designer (the model, part by part) | 4 | 4 | 3.5 |
| First-time viewer (charm on an iPhone) | 4 | 3.5 | 3 |
| Illustrator (the painted finish) | 4 | 3.5 | 3.5 |

## What they still want, most important first

**Character designer (the model, part by part):**
- Crown tuft (head.base outline, top of the head): the crown is one continuous dark line, about RGB 170,127,94 and about 2 px wide at 3x, with shallow, evenly spaced cloud bumps and separate tick commas inside
- Ear (ear.L, side view): it reads as a pasted cut-out
- Line weight and tone overall: the outline on the body, haunch, rump and paws is one dark tone at one weight
- Toe lines on all three paws: there are three dark, evenly spaced strokes per paw (a rake), as dark as the outline, and they read as claws

**First-time viewer (charm on an iPhone):**
- Outline weight and colour (crown, back of head, ear, tail, paw bottoms): the render uses one dark brown line at one weight everywhere (L about 52 to 57 on the crown)
- Crown tuft: the render is one heavy lumpy cap with a single central hump and tiny flick marks
- Eye: the lower iris is almost flat black (lower 40% p90 L 23, sheet 52)
- Ear (ear.L part IoU 0.73): there is a pale highlight band across the ear top that reads as a seam where a separate flap was glued on, and a hard outline down the front edge

**Illustrator (the painted finish):**
- Crown tuft (head top edge): render is one continuous, even-weight wavy outline in a fairly dark brown (luminance ~125, about #A87C5A), with shallow bumps and tiny inward tick marks, so it reads as a cloud or cauliflower stamp
- Eye (iris paint): render iris runs from about #311B0D at the top to #40261A at the bottom, nearly flat and low contrast, which reads as a button
- Tail plume: render is a smooth, tapering feather or flame shape with a pointed tip leaning left, only shallow ripples on the right edge, and long thin parallel arcs inside, so it reads as a feather or fin
- Ear top and root: render ear has a wide, domed top with a hard outline and a pale blurred highlight band along the top edge, which looks like a cap or lid sitting on the head
