# The side view: fresh reviewers' scores

Three fresh reviewers, one lens each, scored the side view against its turnaround crop after the finish pass (spec 5b's rubric, 1 to 5; every line must reach 4 before Gate A). They had not seen earlier rounds.

| Reviewer | On-model | Face | Charm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Character designer (the model, part by part) | 3.5 | 3.5 | 3 |
| First-time viewer (charm on an iPhone) | 4 | 3.5 | 3 |
| Illustrator (the painted finish) | 4 | 4 | 3 |

## What they still want, most important first

**Character designer (the model, part by part):**
- Ear, top and root: the render's top is a clean dome with its own outline and a glossy highlight, so the ear looks pasted on
- Ear, hem and fur strokes: the render's bottom is a smooth arc with tiny regular notches, and its 5-6 parallel fur strokes are evenly spaced like a comb
- Tag: the render's tag is an apple or peach shape with a stem nub on top, an oval inner ring and a heavy dark-brown outline
- Haunch and flank: a straight vertical light/dark seam runs from the collar down to the paws and splits the body into two flat columns

**First-time viewer (charm on an iPhone):**
- Ear (ear.L), the biggest charm loss: the render is a symmetric smooth egg
- Crown (head top / topknot): the render is one continuous, even mid-brown outline
- Nose and upper lip: the render nose is a tilted ellipse, a bean with a pointed back end that pokes into the bridge line, and its highlight is all but invisible
- Eye: the render eye leans about 15 to 20 degrees backward and is rounder

**Illustrator (the painted finish):**
- Ear (ear.L), the dominant shape in this view: it is a symmetric egg with a domed top that sits on the head like a separate object
- Line language: make it one warm brown line everywhere
- Fur edges on the crown tufts: the render draws them as rounded bumps outlined all the way round
- Tail plume: the outline is a uniform line with regular rounded scallops, which looks like a cog or a cloud
