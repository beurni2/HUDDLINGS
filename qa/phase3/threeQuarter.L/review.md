# The three-quarter view: fresh reviewers' scores

Three fresh reviewers, one lens each, scored the three-quarter view against its turnaround crop (spec 5b's rubric, 1 to 5; every line must reach 4 before Gate A). They had not seen earlier rounds.

| Reviewer | On-model | Face | Charm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| First-time viewer (charm on an iPhone) | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| Illustrator (the painted finish) | 3.5 | 3 | 3 |

## What they still want, most important first

**Character designer (the model, part by part):**
- Eyes, both: remove the thin grey or silver rim line inside the iris and the radial lash-like rays in the lower iris
- Near eye: make it slightly oval and about 5% smaller
- Mouth: the cavity is a near-symmetric V or shield that narrows to a point
- Far ear (screen left): it is a flat triangular wedge with a straight diagonal seam where it meets the head, which is the most clip-art-looking edge on the model

**First-time viewer (charm on an iPhone):**
- Mouth (mouth.open, threeQuarter.L): the opening is widest just under the lip and tapers to a near point
- Eyes (both): remove the sharp grey-white rim crescent on the outer edge and the radial starburst spikes in the lower iris
- Ears, painting: replace the evenly spaced straight vertical stripes, which read as corduroy or a curtain, with the sheet's soft painted fur
- Near ear (ear.L), shape: its top edge is a straight diagonal ending in a hard upper-left corner, so it reads as a trapezoid or flag

**Illustrator (the painted finish):**
- Mouth (open, happy): the opening is a narrow downward V or shield that comes to a point under the tongue, which looks more like a yell than a smile
- Eyes: the iris edge is soft-blurred, so both eyes look out of focus beside the sheet's crisp edges
- Outline: one even stroke, mostly a cool grey-taupe (sampled #A48165 at the paw fronts, #B9987C on the far paw, and paler still on the tail and crown)
- Fur edges: the render's silhouette is smooth vector curves almost everywhere
