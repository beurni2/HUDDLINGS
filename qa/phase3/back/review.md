## What they still want, most important first

**Character designer (the model, part by part):**
- TAIL (most important): redraw it as the sheet's curled plume, not an upright mitten
- BACK PATCH SHAPE: the sheet's saddle is a '6' or comma
- HEAD-EAR JUNCTION AND HEAD FUR: on the sheet, the tan at the top of each ear narrows to a point where it meets the crown
- CROWN AND TOPKNOT: the sheet's two topknot lobes stand about 15% taller above the crown line, about 33 px against the render's 28

**First-time viewer (charm on an iPhone):**
- Tail: replace the upright pale lozenge (straight vertical sides, thin thumb-like hook at the top, tan smear inside its left edge) with the sheet's plume
- Ears: the lower edge is one smooth round curve, and the paint is evenly spaced parallel vertical stripes, which reads as a ribbed gourd or eggplant
- Outline everywhere: the line has one even width along its whole length and never breaks, around the crown, the topknot, the rump, the tail and the haunches, so every part looks like a vector sticker
- Body and haunches: the rump reads boxy. The sides are nearly straight and vertical from the collar to the ground, the base is flat with tight corners, and a short stray diagonal crease sits at the bottom of each haunch like a toe line the…

**Illustrator (the painted finish):**
- Tail (biggest problem): the render is a lumpy bean, with random crease lines inside that look like crumpled paper, a tan smudge on its left and no curl
- Haunch bottoms: remove the diagonal crease strokes at both lower outer corners (render around x 85-115 and x 455-490, y 505-520 at 3x)
- Shading and volume: the body and head read as flat, slightly cool cream
- Bottom edge of the head: the render's head has a flat horizontal bottom and straight vertical sides between the ears, like a rectangle
