# The front view: fresh reviewers' scores

Three fresh reviewers, one lens each, scored the final front view against the sheet (spec 5b's rubric, 1 to 5; every line must reach 4 before Gate A). They had not seen earlier rounds.

| Reviewer | On-model | Face | Charm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Character designer (the model, part by part) | 4 | 3.5 | 3.5 |
| First-time viewer (charm on an iPhone) | 4 | 3.5 | 3.5 |
| Illustrator (the painted finish) | 4 | 3.5 | 3 |

## What they still want, most important first

**Character designer (the model, part by part):**
- Eyes (both): rebuild the eye structure to match the sheet
- Ear roots and temples: remove the cut-out seams
- Head patches around the eyes: the top edges are ragged, toothed cut lines and the colour is flat
- Front legs and belly: the legs are straight columns with hard brown inner outlines

**First-time viewer (charm on an iPhone):**
- Eyes (both): rebuild them the way the sheet draws them
- Head patches (head.patch.L/R): the sheet's eye patch is a soft, rounded dome capping the upper and outer eye, with feathered edges
- Front legs and chest: remove the heavy dark inner leg outlines that run the full height to the ground, and the Y crease that starts under the tag
- Paws: the front paws should be round bulbs with convex soles that bulge slightly wider than the leg, not flat-bottomed scalloped mittens that carry on straight down from the leg outline

**Illustrator (the painted finish):**
- Eyes: the thin white arc reads as a reflection; the sheet's white is a clean crescent between the dark rim and the iris on the outer side
- Front legs and chest: flat fills and a strong outline from the collar to the paw read as pillars, with a hard tan wedge under the tag
- Cheek fluff, both sides: an even row of round outlined scallops reads as a cookie-cutter edge, where the sheet breaks it into 3 to 5 irregular clumps
- Ear roots: the tan ends in a hard-cut tab at the top of each ear, where the sheet tucks the ear behind the crown's fur
